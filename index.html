ABOUT OUR COURT DATA
REAL COURTS, REAL SOURCES

The court directory.

Every venue in the initial collection is tied to a government, park operator, tennis federation or other published venue guide. This is a curated launch list, not a full census of tennis courts. Source pages may change.

Photography: Court-specific photos appear only where an identified venue photo URL was available. Each photograph links to its originating source or licence page. Photos remain the property of their creators; verify usage rights before commercial deployment. Courts without a verified venue image show a clearly labelled real photograph of a different tennis venue (representative only). The photo is not claimed to show the listed court.

Privacy: Your wishlist, played courts, badges and reveal history stay in this browser (localStorage). There is no login, backend or tracking; export a JSON backup before clearing browser data or switching devices.

Booking: The prototype does not search available time slots, make reservations, or validate a court's current opening/access. Follow venue sources to confirm.
